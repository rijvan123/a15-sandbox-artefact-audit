## Summary

Every S9 stage after amendment 3 ran on the real data on the authors' PC: the EMBER2024 download, `ember-labels`, `harmonise`, `dryrun` and `freeze-prep`. The pre-registered ClarAVy check (K4) drops agenttesla and formbook and leaves 8, 9 and 8 shared families in origins 1–3 (K3 not triggered). The first dry run was flawed: a bug stopped training after 3 of 5 epochs, and the speed test ran on a busy PC with every thread. Amendment 4 fixed both. The corrected dry run measured K6 far lower (33 h for the amendment 3 plan, not 342 h) but confirmed that K5 is triggered (projected half-width 5.8 points against 2). The authors then decided (amendment 5): margin raised to 3 points, the registered K5 action, and the five registered seeds and 20 search configurations restored, about 59 h projected. The dry run with amendment 5 (5 November, 19:34–20:05) reproduced the model results exactly, projected 58.3 h for the new plan and wrote the freeze bundle. The authors reviewed the bundle and ran `freeze` at 20:13 UTC: the freeze entry (130,774) and its batch are anchored by a trusted RFC 3161 time-stamp, and the whole ledger verifies. The authors filed the OSF Preregistration from the draft in this package; it was approved on 7 November 2026 (DOI 10.17605/OSF.IO/K5EPU), before any model scored a test trace.

All times are UTC. "Measured" means measured on the authors' PC (WSL Ubuntu 24.04, 12 logical CPUs, 7 GB, Python 3.12.16, torch 2.5.1 CPU) unless stated otherwise.

## Why the first dry run (4 November) is superseded

1. **Training stopped early.** `train_deep` applies early stopping after 3 epochs without improvement on the validation set. The dry run has no validation set, so every score was NaN and training always stopped after epoch 3 of 5. The model reached a mean recall of 0.35 and predicted 6 of 9 families; K5 (3.20 points) and P4 (ratio 0.68) were computed from it.
2. **The speed test was not representative.** At sequence length 256 it measured 141 ms (Transformer), 717 ms (BiLSTM) and 12 ms (1D-CNN) per trace: slower than the same models at length 512 on 3 November (92, 292 and 4.7 ms). The PC was busy, PyTorch used all 12 logical CPUs, and only one (the largest) configuration was timed. The projection for the amendment 3 plan was 342 h.

## Amendment 4 (5 November, before the freeze)

- `train_deep` stops early only when a validation set is given; the dry run trains for 10 epochs by default (the epochs the K6 projection assumes).
- The K6 input is the mean cost over the search configurations, with thread count, CPU count and load average recorded.
- `A15_THREADS` sets the PyTorch thread count; `bench_threads.py` finds the fastest setting. It reads no data and changes speed only, not results.
- Thread check, amendment 3 plan, measured: 1 thread 67 h, 2 threads 41 h, 4 threads 48 h, 6 threads 40 h, 8 threads 37 h, 12 threads 41 h. Chosen: 8 threads.

## Authors' decisions = amendment 5 (5 November, before the freeze)

1. **K5.** The test windows cannot widen: the three two-month windows cover March–August 2025, and MELD-DS-448 ends in August 2025. The registered action is applied: the margin Δ becomes 3 points before scoring, and all families are kept. The projected half-width still exceeds 3 points, so equivalence and non-inferiority verdicts may be inconclusive; the registration says so. Δ is the smallest effect of interest in H1–H4, so their support thresholds also become 3 points.
2. **Compute (K6).** The five registered seeds (11, 23, 37, 41, 53) and 20 search configurations are restored for the Transformer (confirmatory) and the 1D-CNN. The BiLSTM is trained unmasked and F-masked only (five seeds); the n-gram LightGBM runs the full factorial; the sequence cap stays at 256. Projected from the corrected dry run: Transformer 37.4 h, BiLSTM 17.6 h, 1D-CNN 3.9 h, about 59 h in total (one run at a time, 8 threads, 10 epochs per run; estimated).

Code changes: `a15/config.py` (Δ 3.0, five seeds, 20 configurations; the K5 limit stays 2.0), `a15/freeze.py` (hypothesis texts take the margin from Δ; settings record `k5_applied` and the compute plan), `a15/gates.py` (K5 record adds the margin in force), `run_s9.py`, `bench_threads.py` and tests (40).

## Limitations to carry into the manuscript

- Label quality: MELD-DS-448 agrees with ClarAVy on 54.5% of confident shared binaries; 7 of the remaining families are unverified; 23 shared binaries are benign in EMBER2024.
- Precision: even with the 3-point margin, equivalence and non-inferiority tests are likely to be underpowered.
- CPU-only design: sequence cap 256 (512 at S8), BiLSTM trained unmasked and F-masked only.
- WinMET has no VirusTotal dates, so it uses the grouped split rather than rolling origins.
- Dry-run numbers come from a pseudo-origin inside the training period and are planning values, not results.
